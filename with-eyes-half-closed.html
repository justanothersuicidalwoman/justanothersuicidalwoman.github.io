«Light makes manifest. But what will manifest the light?» (Laruelle, 1991).

Drawing from the unique perspective of François Laruelle’s non-philosophy, together with philosopher Jacopo Doriano Ostuni , we researched the possibility to question «light in itself» in performance space.

In With eyes half-closed, we share a fragment of an ongoing research, conceived as a philosophical and performative inquiry on light and perception. In a ten days residency at ATD Lectorate we had the chance to start experimenting together the perspectives of François Laruelle, Eduardo Del Estal, and Shihab ad-Din Suhravardi.

In the binary of light and truth and darkness and concealment, there is always a leakage. Even the original light, is darkness, a black immanence. The ink on the paper. 

The darkness is what writes the light; The language, is first thing that is,  a torch in the brain of who writes. That moves deliberately, executing, appearing. And just like light, language dissolves in its context. A few days ago somebody read this quote to me after I mentioned the thought above:

«The first law of subsistence of language is its disappearance from the realm of the visible. In order to be used, it is necessary that language be invisible to us as a code, that we do not think about what language we are in. [...] But at the same time, paradoxically, language is at the origin of the construction of the world.» 

Shihab al-Din Yahya Suhrawardi, known as Shaykh al-Ishraq or The Master of Illumination. In his philosophy, the ultimate truth of knowledge is attained through intuition, inner revelation, and direct presence of light; the essence of existence is 'light,' and the various hierarchies of the cosmos, ranging from the Light of Lights (God) down to darkness and matter, are defined by degrees of intensity and weakness of light.

In his mystical treatise Aql-i Surkh (The Crimson Intellect), Suhrawardi opens with a symbolic narrative: The narrator recounts his primordial origins, saying that before entering this material existence, he lived in a spiritual realm among kindred souls, where they communicated in a shared language. He explains how the trappers of Destiny ensnared him, bringing him to an alien realm such as this. Here, his eyes were sewn shut, bonds were placed upon him, and guards were assigned to him. But over time, his eyes were gradually unbound bit by bit, allowing him to witness things he had never seen before. Finally, his eyes were fully opened, revealing the world as it truly is.

Looking at his heavy bonds and guards, he yearned for the day when these constraints would be lifted, his wings would be freed, and he could fly into the open sky. Eventually, finding the guards heedless, he managed to slip away into the open desert, leading directly to his encounter with the Crimson Intellect. Asking about the color he saw the cCrimson Intellect replies:

« My features are white, I am a luminous elder. But that person who captured you in the snare and placed these disagreeable fetters on you and appointed the wardens over you threw me long ago into a black pit. This color of mine, which appears red to you, is because of that. Otherwise I am white and luminous. Every white thing that is connected to light appears red when mixed with black, like the sunset at the beginning of evening or the end of dawn, which is white where it is connected to the sun's light. One side of it is toward the light, which is white, while the other side is toward the night, which is black. Therefore it appears red. When the crescent moon rises, although its light is borrowed, it is nonetheless described as light. Since one side of it is toward day and the other side toward night, it appears red. A flame has the same quality. Below it is white and above is black smoke. Between the fire and smoke it appears red. There are many such examples."

In this research I look for this crimson color that lays in the middle of light and darkness. Not knowing what is waiting for me at the end.
